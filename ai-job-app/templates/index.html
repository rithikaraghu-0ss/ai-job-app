🧠 How the Risk Score is Calculated

The automation risk score is calculated based on five key factors:

  • Routine Work (30%) – repetitive and structured tasks
  • Repetitive Tasks (25%) – repeated daily operations
  • AI Exposure (25%) – tasks already supported by AI tools
  • Creativity (-10%) – reduces risk because creative work is harder to automate
  • Human Interaction (-10%) – lowers risk due to human decision-making needs
